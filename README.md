# No Peace on Earth

*A Europa Universalis V mod set in the world of Trench Crusade.*

In 1099 the First Crusade took Jerusalem, and the Knights Templar committed the Act of Ultimate Heresy. The Gate of Hell opened beneath the city. Since then the Faithful, Christendom and the Iron Sultanate, have fought a war that never ends. **No Peace on Earth** brings that history into EU5's 1337–1837 timeframe.

> Fan project. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the creators of Trench Crusade or by Paradox Interactive.

## The idea

The mod keeps EU5's 1337 start and 1837 end, and rewrites the world to match the Trench Crusade timeline:

- **A war that never ended.** Hell's realms cannot make peace with the Faithful. Their land can be fought over but never taken: it is heavily fortified, and no human army can hold it.
- **Hell is not united.** The Levant is split into Heretic domains (the Templar Heretics at Acre, the Tyrants of the Cedars, the Lords of Latakia and Moab). Each is ruled by an undying lord and pledged to one of the Seven Heads, and all of them sit in the Court of the Seven-Headed Serpent: Hell's answer to the Church, with its own Infernal Dominion and Decrees of Dis voted by the domains.
- **The Black Grail replaces the Black Death.** In 1346 Beelzebub's plague raises the Grail Host between Zagreb and Zenica, with its own situation, decisions and an army of the risen dead. Corruption spreads out from its land as a second map layer; the dead rise, rebels hand land to the Grail, and fire is the only cure. As it rises, the steppe recoils: the Golden Horde falls back east as the Golden Khanate, the Scythians rise on the Azov coast, Kyiv takes the Dnieper steppe and Moldavia is divided.
- **Bastions of the Faithful.** The Principality of Antioch stands on the front, its Duke funded by every Christian realm. The Iron Sultanate, ruled by the Ottomans from Damascus, holds the Iron Wall from the Taurus to the Gulf; the Wall costs it dearly, its neighbours pay a tithe for its shelter, and when it goes unpaid Hell's slavers come through.
- **Piety vs Heresy.** A new societal value that pulls the Faithful towards Heaven and Hell's realms towards Heresy, and opens the Heretic government reforms.
- **The Balkans before the Fall.** Wallachia rules from Targoviste, Bulgaria joins it as the Crown of the Danube, Athens leads a Hellenic League, and in 1548 they share out Byzantium's Balkan Themes before the Legions of Kimaris take the City.
- **Canon turning points as scripted history.** These include:
  - Joan of Arc and the Battle of Valence (1429)
  - Orichalcum Steel (1503)
  - the destruction of Antioch (1545)
  - Kimaris and the fall of Constantinople (1572)
  - the Order of the Dragon (1573)
  - the Year of Six Woes (1666)
  - the fall of Templar Acre (1703)
  - the Third Siege of New Antioch (1721)
  - Nelson and the Bloodied Cliffs (1805)
  - the Basilisk Fleet in Éire (1807)

**Lore fidelity is a hard rule.** Canon dates and outcomes are fixed. Anything canon does not cover is marked *(invented)* in the design docs, so it can be replaced if the setting fills the gap.

## Status

Early development (v0.1, EU5 1.4). Most of the design is scripted and tested in long observer runs, but the mod content is not published in this repository yet. For now the repository holds the design:

- [`docs/mod-design.md`](docs/mod-design.md): the full design, with phases, canon timeline, implementation status, engine notes and lore checks.
- [`docs/mod-content.md`](docs/mod-content.md): the content catalogue.

## Looking for collaborators

This is a big setting for one person. Help is very welcome in any of these areas:

- **Lore.** Check the design against Trench Crusade canon, fill gaps, and catch mistakes. Sources such as the rulebook or trenchcrusade.com are especially valuable.
- **EU5 scripting.** Write events, situations, international organizations, AI behaviour and balance.
- **Art.** Make event illustrations and modifier icons (the new countries' flags are done; see [`docs/art-requests.md`](docs/art-requests.md) for what is still needed).
- **Writing and localization.** Write event text in the setting's voice, and translate it into other languages.
- **Playtesting.** Play long campaigns and report what breaks, what feels wrong, and what is missing.

**How to join:** open an [issue](../../issues) to suggest an idea, report a lore problem or say what you would like to work on. Discussion is welcome before code. If you love the setting and EU5, there is a place for you here.
